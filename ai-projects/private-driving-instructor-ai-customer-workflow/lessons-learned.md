# Lessons Learned

## Business lesson
AI should not be placed into a business process before the process itself is understood and defined.

## Systems lesson
A reliable workflow needs explicit inputs, processing steps, validation, decision rules, outputs, actions, and human handoff conditions.

## AI reliability lesson
AI can mislead a customer when approved questions, approved answers, eligibility guidelines, exception rules, and process boundaries are not defined.

## Important principle
**EXTRACT ≠ VERIFY**

A customer statement is not automatically verified evidence.

## Human-in-the-loop lesson
AI should not invent business policy. When information is sensitive, conflicting, uncertain, or outside approved rules, human judgment is required.
