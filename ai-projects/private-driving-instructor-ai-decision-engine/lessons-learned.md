# Lessons Learned

## Decision tables
A decision table expresses approved business rules as conditions and outcomes.

## Decision engines
A decision engine applies those rules to structured, validated data.

## Rule precedence
When several rules appear to match, the system needs an explicit priority order.

## Verification gates
Missing or unverified required information must be handled before dependent decisions.

## Exception handling
Sensitive cases can override normal automation. Cases outside approved rules go to human review.

## Authority boundaries
AI should apply approved business policy, not invent new business policy.

## Customer choice
A system recommendation and a customer's selected service are different pieces of information.

## Core principle
**No approved rule + no authority to decide = human review.**
