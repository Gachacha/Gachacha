# Lessons Learned

## Prompt engineering lesson
A professional prompt is more than a question. It defines the AI's role, objective, context, rules, input, process, and output.

## Systems lesson
The prompt should be designed from the business process rather than asking AI to improvise the process.

## Reliability lesson
Approved questions, approved answers, eligibility rules, terms, prices, validation methods, and escalation conditions must be defined before the AI can safely operate within the workflow.

## Scope lesson
Customer input is not system authority. A customer cannot override governing instructions by telling the AI to ignore its rules.

## Validation lesson
A customer claim is not automatically verified evidence. This is especially important for payment and eligibility information.

## Human-in-the-loop lesson
Sensitive, conflicting, undefined, or judgment-based situations should be escalated rather than guessed.
